Our Story

Where Expertise Creates Impact

Value Connect began as a management consultancy in Dubai and has since expanded its presence through incorporation in Kenya. The company was established to create opportunities, unlock growth, and foster meaningful business partnerships between the United Arab Emirates and Africa, including through major international platforms such as EXPO 2020.

Today, Value Connect supports clients across a broad range of sectors, including services, tourism, manufacturing, real estate, fast-moving consumer goods (FMCG), government, and non-governmental organizations. Our network spans industry associations, chambers of commerce, government institutions, diplomatic missions, private-sector organizations, and international partners across Africa, the Middle East, and Europe. These include the Kenya Association of Manufacturers (KAM), Kenya National Chamber of Commerce and Industry (KNCCI), Dubai Chamber of Commerce, Kenya Investment Authority, Kenya Airways, KEPSA, VDMA Germany, and other leading organizations.

As a privately held company, Value Connect is agile, flexible, and able to tailor its approach to the specific objectives of each client. We combine professional expertise, integrity, and strong market relationships to deliver solutions that go beyond conventional industry expectations.

At the heart of everything we do is one principle: value addition. If we cannot add value to your business, then we have no business being in your business.

Above all, we are committed to creating sustainable linkages that connect people, businesses, markets, and opportunities across borders.
