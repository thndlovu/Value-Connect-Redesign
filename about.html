Our Story

Where Expertise Creates Impact

Value Connect began as a management consultancy in Dubai and was incorporated in Kenya in 2018. It was established to create opportunities, unlock growth, and build meaningful business partnerships, initially through trade missions and access to export markets. Over time, the company evolved to place greater emphasis on leadership, communication, personal development, and team development.

Today, Value Connect works with organizations to strengthen leadership, culture, communication, customer relationships, and business growth. Through customized training and development, the company helps clients build stronger teams, develop more effective leaders, and create the capabilities needed to perform in changing and complex business environments. Its work is supported by Maxwell Leadership resources and an approach grounded in practical application and measurable value.

Value Connect maintains strong relationships within the business community, including its involvement with the Kenya Association of Manufacturers in the Services Sector and the American Chamber of Commerce. These connections support ongoing engagement with manufacturers, businesses, institutions, and leaders across sectors.

The company has worked across a broad range of industries, including services, tourism, manufacturing, real estate, fast-moving consumer goods, government, and non-governmental organizations. Its client experience includes organizations such as Absa Bank Kenya, EABL-Diageo, Fidelity Insurance, Liaison Group, Sanlam Insurance, Saruni Basecamp Explorer, Safaricom, Isuzu, Oracle, Kevian, Zamara, KCB Bank, Centum, and Farmers Choice.

As a privately held consultancy, Value Connect brings an agile and tailored approach to every engagement. International experience, strong professional relationships, and a collaborative team approach allow the company to draw on global best practices while responding to the specific needs and objectives of each client.

At the centre of Value Connect is a commitment to value addition — delivering work that strengthens people, organizations, and relationships in a meaningful and sustainable way.

Above all, Value Connect exists to create connections that move people and businesses forward: connecting leaders to better ways of working, organizations to new opportunities, and people to the relationships that make sustainable growth possible.
