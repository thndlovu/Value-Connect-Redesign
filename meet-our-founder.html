Biography

Mercy brings more than 20 years of experience working across three continents and five countries, helping senior executive teams from different cultures navigate complex environments, strengthen collaboration, and achieve meaningful results. That experience became the foundation for Value Connect Management Consultancy Ltd., where she continues to focus on helping leaders and teams work more effectively together.

At the centre of her approach is a belief that people are the most important part of any organization. As a certified Behavioral Consultant, Mercy uses proven tools to help teams communicate more effectively, reduce conflict, strengthen relationships, and create the conditions for better performance.

Her work is focused particularly on managers and executives. By combining her professional experience with leadership development and behavioral insight, she helps leaders achieve their desired outcomes while strengthening the way they lead. As an appointed Maxwell Leadership Executive Director, she also has access to specialized leadership resources built around the principle that everyone deserves to be led well.

Mercy is the author of Three’s Divine and has received international recognition for her work, including the Maxwell Live2Lead Global 2020 Award and a World Women Leadership Award in 2019 for her contributions in the Middle East.

She holds a Master’s degree in Leadership & Sustainability from the University of Cumbria in the United Kingdom. She also serves as Chair for the Services Sector at the Kenya Association of Manufacturers (KAM) and is a member of the Women in Manufacturing (WIM) steering committee.

Through Value Connect, Mercy continues to bring together leadership, experience, and relationships with one clear objective: to serve, create meaningful connections, and add lasting value to the people and organizations she works with.
